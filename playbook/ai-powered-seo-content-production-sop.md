# AI-Powered SEO Content Production Playbook / SOP

## Purpose

This playbook explains how to build an AI-assisted SEO content production workflow based on the research collected in this repository.

It is based on expert interviews, YouTube transcripts, LinkedIn posts, and source annotations collected during the research stage.

## Who This Playbook Is For

This playbook is designed for B2B SaaS teams that want to use AI to improve SEO content production without sacrificing quality, authority, originality, or long-term search visibility.

## Core Principles

These principles guide every stage of the SOP below. Each one is grounded in the research corpus; syntheses cite all relevant sources.

### 1. AI-assisted content production is a workflow problem, not a prompt problem

Strong output depends on structured, multi-step processes—research, outlining, drafting, verification, and review—not on a single generation prompt. Break work into discrete steps, save the output of each step, and refine the workflow over time so you can trace failures back to a specific stage. (source: Ryan Law, How to automate blog writing with AI from keyword to published, April 28, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVZrVeESnFQ) (source: Ryan Law, AI Content Wasn't Good Enough. Now It Is., March 16, 2026, https://www.linkedin.com/posts/thinkingslow_ai-content-wasnt-good-enough-now-it-is-activity-7439350229327302656-3JNC)

### 2. Keep a human in the loop for strategy, judgment, and final accountability

AI can remove repetitive drafting and research work, but humans must set strategy, provide original context, validate claims, and decide what gets published. The effective model is closer to editor-in-chief than fully automated publishing. (source: Kevin Indig, Moving Beyond Old SEO Models in the Age of AI, January 19, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AqAoKGftsSE) (source: Ryan Law, AI Content Wasn't Good Enough. Now It Is., March 16, 2026, https://www.linkedin.com/posts/thinkingslow_ai-content-wasnt-good-enough-now-it-is-activity-7439350229327302656-3JNC) (source: Patrick Stox, Patrick Stox: GEO? AEO? LLMO? What's With All This AI SEO Stuff?, November 21, 2025, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vNFMdcw4uwo)

### 3. Traditional SEO foundations still matter for AI search visibility

Crawlability, indexability, site health, information architecture, and content quality remain prerequisites. Short-term AI-only tactics that weaken Google performance can also reduce AI citations over time. (source: Aleyda Solís, 202: Aleyda Solís: AI search crawlability and why your site's tech foundations decide visibility, January 13, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pqrwpXpMM6s) (source: Lily Ray, The Worst Thing You Can Do for AI Search Visibility Is Destroy Your SEO, June 2, 2026, https://www.linkedin.com/posts/lily-ray-44755615_the-worst-thing-you-can-do-for-your-ai-search-activity-7467554630458093568-81Xg) (source: Lily Ray, Google's 2026 Crackdown: Lily Ray Says Your SEO Strategy is Failing, March 25, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-pUMNtq8Bp0)

### 4. Optimize for topics and business outcomes, not clicks alone

Website traffic is an incomplete KPI in AI search. Track brand mentions, AI citations, branded search, self-attributed discovery, and pipeline impact alongside traditional metrics. Communicate this shift to leadership so resources align with influence, not just volume. (source: Bernard Huang, Why Traffic Isn't the KPI Anymore (AI Search Shift Explained), March 3, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=10tXsYBEvw4) (source: Steve Toth, AI SEO After the Hype: What Actually Works in 2026, January 22, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ohPxcZJbQi8) (source: Kevin Indig, AI SEO Adoption Requires Change Management, February 26, 2026, https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kevinindig_want-an-8x-success-multiplier-on-your-ai-activity-7432767721030754306-iorn)

### 5. Think in topics and intent clusters, not isolated prompts or keywords

Prompts are long, noisy, and highly personalized. Prioritize on-brand topics, product lines, and customer-journey areas where you need visibility, then use sample prompts only to track directional change over time. (source: Aleyda Solís, 202: Aleyda Solís: AI search crawlability and why your site's tech foundations decide visibility, January 13, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pqrwpXpMM6s) (source: Kevin Indig, Moving Beyond Old SEO Models in the Age of AI, January 19, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AqAoKGftsSE)

### 6. Differentiation beats generic volume

Content that adds original research, proprietary data, case studies, product use cases, or expert perspective outperforms scaled generic output. Reserve full automation for well-covered informational topics where you already have internal source material; keep high-stakes or strategic pieces human-led. (source: Ross Hudgens, AI Visibility, Data Journalism, and the Future of SEO, December 11, 2025, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8-PS7gR2G0I) (source: Ryan Law, How to automate blog writing with AI from keyword to published, April 28, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVZrVeESnFQ) (source: Tim Soulo, Tim Soulo: Where Marketing's Headed And How Ahrefs is Evolving, November 21, 2025, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1J26dKqzzgg)

### 7. Visibility requires presence across the open web, not only on your site

AI systems look for consensus from multiple authoritative sources. Earn citations and brand mentions on third-party platforms—LinkedIn, YouTube, Reddit, reviews, PR, and industry publications—in addition to optimizing owned pages. (source: Mike King, Ranking in Google's AI Results in 2026, January 7, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fKJ18NSHzCE) (source: Michael King, Google's Guidance on AI Search Is Naive and Self-Serving, May 18, 2026, https://www.linkedin.com/posts/michaelkingphilly_googles-guidance-on-ai-search-is-naive-and-activity-7462205788187484160-gxDd) (source: Ross Hudgens, AI Visibility, Data Journalism, and the Future of SEO, December 11, 2025, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8-PS7gR2G0I)

### 8. AI search platforms are not interchangeable

Different models retrieve, cite, and summarize differently. Evaluate visibility across Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini, and other relevant platforms rather than optimizing for one engine only. (source: Bernard Huang, AI Search Engines Are Not All the Same, March 4, 2026, https://www.linkedin.com/posts/bernardjhuang_everyone-keeps-saying-ai-is-the-new-search-activity-7435061789035253761-lv81) (source: Patrick Stox, GEO, AEO, LLMO, and AI SEO Research, October 21, 2025, https://www.linkedin.com/posts/patrickstox_geo-aeo-llmo-whats-with-all-this-ai-stuff-activity-7386441351078256640-JYW8)

### 9. Technical accessibility determines whether AI can use your content at all

Ensure AI crawlers can access your content, avoid relying on client-side JavaScript for critical text and navigation, and verify that hosting or CDN settings are not blocking AI bots by default. Structure content so both full documents and individual sections can be retrieved. (source: Aleyda Solís, 202: Aleyda Solís: AI search crawlability and why your site's tech foundations decide visibility, January 13, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pqrwpXpMM6s) (source: Aleyda Solís, Top SEO & AI Search Updates from SEOFOMO, January 25, 2026, https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aleyda_top-seo-ai-search-updates-from-seofomo-activity-7421667744351260673-UIb3) (source: Patrick Stox, Patrick Stox: GEO? AEO? LLMO? What's With All This AI SEO Stuff?, November 21, 2025, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vNFMdcw4uwo)

### 10. Scale without quality control is a liability, not an advantage

Mass AI-generated publishing, thin doorway pages, and manipulative listicles create footprint patterns that search systems increasingly penalize. Recovery from algorithmic hits can take months or years. (source: Lily Ray, Google's 2026 Crackdown: Lily Ray Says Your SEO Strategy is Failing, March 25, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-pUMNtq8Bp0) (source: Steve Toth, AI SEO After the Hype: What Actually Works in 2026, January 22, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ohPxcZJbQi8)

### 11. B2B buyers evaluate fit inside AI systems—your content must answer deal-breaker questions

For B2B SaaS, AI visibility is not only about being cited. Buyers use AI to compare integrations, pricing, compliance, ICP fit, and alternatives before visiting your site. Landing pages and comparison content must answer those qualifying questions explicitly. (source: Steve Toth, B2B AEO Beyond "Just SEO", June 5, 2026, https://www.linkedin.com/posts/stevetothjr_getting-cited-in-ai-overviews-is-just-seo-activity-7468647893676232704-VpE_) (source: Steve Toth, AI SEO After the Hype: What Actually Works in 2026, January 22, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ohPxcZJbQi8)

### 12. Treat AI SEO adoption as organizational change, not a tool rollout

Pilot workflows, expand what works, embed standards into editorial and technical processes, and translate AI visibility into business language leadership can act on. (source: Kevin Indig, AI SEO Adoption Requires Change Management, February 26, 2026, https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kevinindig_want-an-8x-success-multiplier-on-your-ai-activity-7432767721030754306-iorn) (source: Ross Hudgens, How Content Teams Should Approach AI Adoption, May 28, 2026, https://www.linkedin.com/posts/rosshudgens_ceocmo-do-we-use-profound-are-we-using-activity-7465776563909890049-fHjh)

## Step-by-Step SOP

Use this SOP for each new article or major content update. Steps are sequential; skipping research or human review increases the risk of generic, unverifiable, or off-brand output.

### Step 1 — Define the Search and Business Goal

**Purpose:** Tie every content piece to a measurable business outcome before any AI-assisted work begins.

1. **State the business goal first.** Define what success looks like beyond publishing—e.g., pipeline influence, product adoption, branded recall, or support deflection—not merely "create content." (source: Tim Soulo, Tim Soulo: Where Marketing's Headed And How Ahrefs is Evolving, November 21, 2025, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1J26dKqzzgg)

2. **Identify the primary audience and buying stage.** For B2B SaaS, note whether the piece supports discovery, evaluation, or decision-stage research. B2B buyers increasingly run deep comparisons inside AI tools before visiting vendor sites. (source: Steve Toth, AI SEO After the Hype: What Actually Works in 2026, January 22, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ohPxcZJbQi8)

3. **Choose KPIs beyond traffic.** Pair traditional metrics (rankings, clicks, conversions) with AI visibility signals: brand mentions, citation frequency, branded search, and self-attributed discovery (e.g., "Where did you hear about us?"). (source: Bernard Huang, Why Traffic Isn't the KPI Anymore (AI Search Shift Explained), March 3, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=10tXsYBEvw4) (source: Patrick Stox, Patrick Stox: GEO? AEO? LLMO? What's With All This AI SEO Stuff?, November 21, 2025, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vNFMdcw4uwo)

4. **Document deal-breaker questions for B2B pages.** List the qualifying criteria your ICP uses—integrations, pricing model, security, company size fit, support, compliance—and ensure the content brief addresses them. Reverse-engineer ChatGPT Deep Research or Gemini planning prompts to surface the questions AI systems ask before recommending vendors. (source: Steve Toth, AI SEO After the Hype: What Actually Works in 2026, January 22, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ohPxcZJbQi8) (source: Steve Toth, B2B AEO Beyond "Just SEO", June 5, 2026, https://www.linkedin.com/posts/stevetothjr_getting-cited-in-ai-overviews-is-just-seo-activity-7468647893676232704-VpE_)

5. **Confirm brand and topic fit before proceeding.** Reject topics with high search volume but poor alignment with product, positioning, or ICP—even if AI could draft them quickly. (source: Aleyda Solís, 202: Aleyda Solís: AI search crawlability and why your site's tech foundations decide visibility, January 13, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pqrwpXpMM6s)

**Output:** A one-page brief with business goal, audience, primary topic, success metrics, and deal-breaker questions to answer.

---

### Step 2 — Select Topics Based on Search Demand, Brand Fit, and AI Visibility Potential

**Purpose:** Prioritize topics where you can win in both traditional search and AI retrieval without diluting brand focus.

1. **Start from bottom- and mid-funnel topics for B2B.** Prioritize pricing, comparisons, alternatives, case studies, and solution pages over generic top-of-funnel definitions that AI can answer without a click. (source: Ross Hudgens, AI Visibility, Data Journalism, and the Future of SEO, December 11, 2025, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8-PS7gR2G0I)

2. **Map query fan-out, not just head terms.** For each target topic, identify the subqueries AI systems generate in the background—related questions, format expectations (FAQ, comparison table, checklist), and implicit intent. Use fan-out research to decide whether one page or multiple supporting pages are needed. (source: Mike King, Ranking in Google's AI Results in 2026, January 7, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fKJ18NSHzCE) (source: Bernard Huang, Why Traffic Isn't the KPI Anymore (AI Search Shift Explained), March 3, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=10tXsYBEvw4)

3. **Prioritize high-frequency fan-out queries when budget is limited.** Focus content investment on web searches that recur consistently at low temperature (deterministic) settings; treat long-tail one-off fan-outs selectively. (source: Bernard Huang, Why Traffic Isn't the KPI Anymore (AI Search Shift Explained), March 3, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=10tXsYBEvw4)

4. **Validate topics against brand fit and YMYL risk.** Apply stricter human oversight for legal, financial, security, or health-adjacent SaaS topics; Google scrutinizes AI-assisted content more aggressively in sensitive categories. (source: Lily Ray, Google's 2026 Crackdown: Lily Ray Says Your SEO Strategy is Failing, March 25, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-pUMNtq8Bp0)

5. **Check topic visibility by product line or use case, not generic brand queries.** Configure tracking around the specific topics where you compete (e.g., "project management for agencies") rather than obsessing over individual prompt wording. (source: Aleyda Solís, 202: Aleyda Solís: AI search crawlability and why your site's tech foundations decide visibility, January 13, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pqrwpXpMM6s)

6. **Involve sales, support, and product in topic selection.** Mine customer conversations, support tickets, and sales calls for questions not yet visible in keyword tools—especially for LLM-driven research where prompts rarely repeat verbatim. (source: Aleyda Solís, 202: Aleyda Solís: AI search crawlability and why your site's tech foundations decide visibility, January 13, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pqrwpXpMM6s) (source: Kevin Indig, Moving Beyond Old SEO Models in the Age of AI, January 19, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AqAoKGftsSE)

7. **Flag automation candidates separately from strategic pieces.** Mark topics suitable for AI-heavy production (well-covered informational subjects where you already have internal references) versus topics requiring original insight, data, or executive perspective. (source: Ryan Law, How to automate blog writing with AI from keyword to published, April 28, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVZrVeESnFQ)

**Output:** A prioritized topic list with fan-out subtopics, intended page format, funnel stage, automation tier, and primary success metric.

### Step 3 — Build a Research Packet Before Writing

**Purpose:** Give AI (and human writers) verified inputs so output is accurate, differentiated, and on-brand.

1. **Analyze top-ranking SERP content for consensus and gaps.** Fetch top results, extract headers and themes, cross-reference overlapping ideas, and document information gain opportunities the SERP does not cover. (source: Ryan Law, How to automate blog writing with AI from keyword to published, April 28, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVZrVeESnFQ)

2. **Pull keyword, parent-topic, and question data through your SEO stack.** Include related keywords, People Also Ask themes (grouped by intent, not FAQ spam), and dominant SERP content formats. (source: Ryan Law, How to automate blog writing with AI from keyword to published, April 28, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVZrVeESnFQ)

3. **Audit existing owned content on the topic.** Search your site for related articles, product use cases, and internal messaging so the new piece stays consistent, avoids duplication, and supports internal linking. (source: Ryan Law, How to automate blog writing with AI from keyword to published, April 28, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVZrVeESnFQ) (source: Kevin Indig, Moving Beyond Old SEO Models in the Age of AI, January 19, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AqAoKGftsSE)

4. **Add proprietary inputs where possible.** Include original data, customer examples, product screenshots, case study excerpts, or internal documentation—content that generic LLM training cannot replicate. (source: Ross Hudgens, AI Visibility, Data Journalism, and the Future of SEO, December 11, 2025, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8-PS7gR2G0I) (source: Lily Ray, Google's 2026 Crackdown: Lily Ray Says Your SEO Strategy is Failing, March 25, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-pUMNtq8Bp0)

5. **Review third-party sources that currently influence AI answers.** Identify citations and brand mentions in AI responses for your target topic; note inaccurate or missing representations that outreach, PR, or new content should correct. (source: Bernard Huang, Why Traffic Isn't the KPI Anymore (AI Search Shift Explained), March 3, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=10tXsYBEvw4) (source: Tim Soulo, Tim Soulo: Where Marketing's Headed And How Ahrefs is Evolving, November 21, 2025, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1J26dKqzzgg)

6. **Compile a single research packet document.** Merge SERP analysis, keyword data, owned-content references, gap list, and source URLs into one file that feeds the outline and drafting steps. (source: Ryan Law, How to automate blog writing with AI from keyword to published, April 28, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVZrVeESnFQ)

**Output:** A research packet (markdown or doc) with SERP themes, gaps, keyword clusters, internal links to include, and citations to verify.

### Step 4 — Use AI for Drafting, Structuring, and Acceleration

**Purpose:** Use AI to speed up structured production while keeping process control—not to replace editorial judgment.

1. **Define a multi-step workflow with saved intermediate outputs.** Typical stages: research → outline → product/context annotation → draft → claim verification → internal linking → format for CMS. Run steps individually when debugging; batch only after the workflow is tested. (source: Ryan Law, How to automate blog writing with AI from keyword to published, April 28, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVZrVeESnFQ)

2. **Provide directional context at workflow start.** Before generation, supply free-form notes on angle, audience, product mentions, and sections to include or exclude. This is often what makes the piece unique. (source: Ryan Law, How to automate blog writing with AI from keyword to published, April 28, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVZrVeESnFQ)

3. **Anchor generation to examples, not abstract style rules alone.** Feed AI prior outlines, published posts, and engagement examples so it infers voice from real samples rather than from a short bullet list of "brand voice" traits. (source: Ryan Law, How to automate blog writing with AI from keyword to published, April 28, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVZrVeESnFQ)

4. **Structure outlines for clarity and retrieval.** Use mutually exclusive sections, logical header flow, BLUF (bottom line up front) openings per section, and explicit examples. (source: Ryan Law, How to automate blog writing with AI from keyword to published, April 28, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVZrVeESnFQ) (source: Steve Toth, AI SEO After the Hype: What Actually Works in 2026, January 22, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ohPxcZJbQi8)

5. **Use AI for ideation and sparring on commoditized sections only.** Let AI draft repetitive explanatory paragraphs when humans would rather spend time on data, product context, or narrative—but keep reviewers accountable for the final text. (source: Ross Hudgens, How Content Teams Should Approach AI Adoption, May 28, 2026, https://www.linkedin.com/posts/rosshudgens_ceocmo-do-we-use-profound-are-we-using-activity-7465776563909890049-fHjh) (source: Ross Hudgens, AI Visibility, Data Journalism, and the Future of SEO, December 11, 2025, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8-PS7gR2G0I)

6. **Maintain a brand source-of-truth file.** Store positioning, product facts, approved use cases, and proof points in a shared document AI steps can reference—equivalent to a structured brand kit. (source: Ross Hudgens, How Content Teams Should Approach AI Adoption, May 28, 2026, https://www.linkedin.com/posts/rosshudgens_ceocmo-do-we-use-profound-are-we-using-activity-7465776563909890049-fHjh) (source: Tim Soulo, Tim Soulo: Where Marketing's Headed And How Ahrefs is Evolving, November 21, 2025, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1J26dKqzzgg)

7. **Iterate and simplify the workflow over time.** Test whether each skill/prompt step still adds value as models improve; remove steps that no longer change output meaningfully. (source: Ryan Law, How to automate blog writing with AI from keyword to published, April 28, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVZrVeESnFQ)

**Output:** Structured outline, annotated draft, and step-by-step artifacts stored for review and reuse.

### Step 5 — Add Human Judgment, Originality, and Brand Context

**Purpose:** Ensure the published piece reflects real expertise, accurate claims, and differentiated B2B SaaS positioning.

1. **Review every AI draft before publication.** Do not set-and-forget. Human oversight is required to confirm accuracy, originality, and value beyond what already exists online. (source: Lily Ray, Google's 2026 Crackdown: Lily Ray Says Your SEO Strategy is Failing, March 25, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-pUMNtq8Bp0) (source: Patrick Stox, Patrick Stox: GEO? AEO? LLMO? What's With All This AI SEO Stuff?, November 21, 2025, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vNFMdcw4uwo)

2. **Verify claims and update statistics.** Run a dedicated fact-check pass: identify assertions requiring sources, replace outdated stats, and add citations for anything that could mislead buyers or trigger trust penalties. (source: Ryan Law, How to automate blog writing with AI from keyword to published, April 28, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVZrVeESnFQ)

3. **Inject non-replicable context.** Add founder/expert commentary, customer stories, product workflows, screenshots, and opinions that AI cannot infer from public SERP content alone. (source: Tim Soulo, Tim Soulo: Where Marketing's Headed And How Ahrefs is Evolving, November 21, 2025, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1J26dKqzzgg) (source: Kevin Indig, Moving Beyond Old SEO Models in the Age of AI, January 19, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AqAoKGftsSE)

4. **Dictate or brain-dump strategic angles for high-value pieces.** For original thought leadership, record unstructured notes and resources, then let AI organize and draft—keeping the ideas human-owned. (source: Ryan Law, How to automate blog writing with AI from keyword to published, April 28, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVZrVeESnFQ)

5. **Personalize workflows by writer or team.** Fork shared automation templates so each author uses their own voice examples and editorial preferences rather than a one-size-fits-all pipeline. (source: Ryan Law, How to automate blog writing with AI from keyword to published, April 28, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iVZrVeESnFQ)

6. **Decline to publish when the piece lacks information gain.** If the draft repeats SERP consensus without product-specific or data-driven differentiation, send it back for more research or kill it. (source: Ross Hudgens, AI Visibility, Data Journalism, and the Future of SEO, December 11, 2025, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8-PS7gR2G0I) (source: Lily Ray, Google's 2026 Crackdown: Lily Ray Says Your SEO Strategy is Failing, March 25, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-pUMNtq8Bp0)

**Output:** Editor-reviewed draft with verified claims, original elements highlighted, and explicit sign-off from a human owner.

### Step 6 — Optimize for Traditional SEO and AI Search Retrieval

**Purpose:** Make the page crawlable, citable, and useful in both blue-link search and AI-generated answers.

1. **Confirm AI crawlers can access the page.** Audit robots.txt, CDN/firewall rules, and hosting defaults to ensure AI bots are not blocked; verify critical content is available in server-rendered HTML, not only via client-side JavaScript. (source: Aleyda Solís, 202: Aleyda Solís: AI search crawlability and why your site's tech foundations decide visibility, January 13, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pqrwpXpMM6s) (source: Patrick Stox, Patrick Stox: GEO? AEO? LLMO? What's With All This AI SEO Stuff?, November 21, 2025, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vNFMdcw4uwo)

2. **Write retrievable intro and conclusion blocks.** Open with a compact summary of what the page answers; close with a clear wrap-up. Place key comparison data in scannable tables where appropriate. LLMs weight beginning and end of documents heavily. (source: Steve Toth, AI SEO After the Hype: What Actually Works in 2026, January 22, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ohPxcZJbQi8)

3. **Cover related subtopics across the site, not only on one page.** Build supporting pages or sections for fan-out queries; use internal links to connect topic clusters. (source: Mike King, Ranking in Google's AI Results in 2026, January 7, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fKJ18NSHzCE) (source: Bernard Huang, Why Traffic Isn't the KPI Anymore (AI Search Shift Explained), March 3, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=10tXsYBEvw4)

4. **Make ICP and use-case targeting explicit.** Include "who it's for" sections, comparison pages with current dates where relevant, and landing-page copy that states integrations, pricing model, and fit criteria clearly. (source: Steve Toth, AI SEO After the Hype: What Actually Works in 2026, January 22, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ohPxcZJbQi8)

5. **Use one idea per paragraph and logical section flow.** Avoid long unstructured middle sections where retrieval systems lose context. (source: Steve Toth, AI SEO After the Hype: What Actually Works in 2026, January 22, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ohPxcZJbQi8) (source: Aleyda Solís, Top SEO & AI Search Updates from SEOFOMO, January 25, 2026, https://www.linkedin.com/posts/aleyda_top-seo-ai-search-updates-from-seofomo-activity-7421667744351260673-UIb3)

6. **Prioritize on-brand informational content over generic glossary pages.** Invest in industry insights, research, case studies, and decision-support content—even when AI Overviews reduce clicks—because it builds authority and personalization-resilient visibility. (source: Aleyda Solís, 202: Aleyda Solís: AI search crawlability and why your site's tech foundations decide visibility, January 13, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pqrwpXpMM6s)

7. **Do not create separate content solely for LLMs.** Optimize one high-quality page for both Google and AI retrieval unless a fan-out query justifies a dedicated page with no traditional search volume. (source: Steve Toth, AI SEO After the Hype: What Actually Works in 2026, January 22, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ohPxcZJbQi8)

8. **Plan off-site reinforcement.** Schedule LinkedIn posts, YouTube clips, community participation, or PR that repeats core messaging on third-party surfaces AI systems cite. (source: Lily Ray, Google's 2026 Crackdown: Lily Ray Says Your SEO Strategy is Failing, March 25, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-pUMNtq8Bp0) (source: Patrick Stox, Patrick Stox: GEO? AEO? LLMO? What's With All This AI SEO Stuff?, November 21, 2025, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vNFMdcw4uwo)

**Output:** Publish-ready page with technical checks passed, retrieval-friendly structure, internal links, and an off-site amplification plan.

### Step 7 — Publish, Measure, and Update

**Purpose:** Close the loop with measurement that reflects AI search reality and keep content fresh.

1. **Track traditional and AI visibility together.** Monitor rankings, clicks, conversions, AI citations, brand mentions, and branded search.
